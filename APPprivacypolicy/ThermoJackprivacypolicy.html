ThermoJack Privacy Policy

ThermoJack does not collect any user's personal data. We do not require users to get registered, nor keeping track of the consumers history of use.

Microphone and storage will be accessed only after getting user's premission of use. User can turn off the corresponding premissions of access in Phone's setting.

We used below android.permissions for the normal running of the APP :

(1) android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO : to allow our APP to access phone's microphone for sending and receiving audio signal from our product ThermoJack

(2) android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE : to allow APP data to be saved to phone's memory

(3) android.permission.BLUETOOTH : to enable audio jack function that allows our product ThermoJack connection for sending and receiving audio signal
