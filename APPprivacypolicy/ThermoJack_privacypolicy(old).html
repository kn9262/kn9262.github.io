Privacy Policy

Our APP ThermoJack does not collect any user's personal data. We do not require users to get registered, nor keeping track of the consumers history of use.

We used below android.permissions for the normal running of the APP :

(1) android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO : to allow our APP to access phone's microphone for sending and receiving signal from our product ThermoJack

(2) android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE : to allow APP data to be saved into phone's memory

(3) android.permission.BLUETOOTH : to enable audio jack function
