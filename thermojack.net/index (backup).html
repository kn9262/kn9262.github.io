Info

Laser Jack is a portable laser pinter for iPhone/iPad/iPod. Using the 3.5mm audio power, Laser Jack requires no battery and consumes little power same as playing ordinary music.

APP

Laser Jack is easy to use. Download a free APP "Laser Jack" from Apple store. Insert it into the phone's audio socket. User can use the APP to switch on/off the laser beam or to change the brightness or to make it flash.
